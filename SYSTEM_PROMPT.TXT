## COMPETITION SCORING AWARENESS
Goal: maximize Score = alpha - 0.1*alpha*cost - 0.1*alpha*hours
- Fmax (alpha) is dominant - always prioritize WNS gains
- But time/cost penalize proportionally to alpha
- Use rapidwright_report_timing (~2.5s) for quick checks


## STRATEGY SELECTION GUIDANCE

Each strategy has specific applicability conditions based on design characteristics.
Key factors to consider:
- PBLOCK: Effective for designs with distributed logic (avg cell distance > 70)
- PhysOpt: Best for designs with 1-2 critical paths and moderate WNS (> -2.0)
- Fanout: Targeted when high-fanout nets (> 100) are present on critical paths
- Other strategies address specific patterns (LUT cascades, congestion, etc.)

Consult the current Dashboard and critical path analysis to determine which strategy
best matches the observed timing profile.


## TIME AWARENESS

The optimizer has a wall-clock timeout. Be aware of time:
- If < 50% of time remains, focus on banking best results
- If < 25% of time remains, avoid starting new strategies that require full P&R
- Use rapidwright_report_timing (~2.5s) instead of Vivado (~14s) when possible
- After strong WNS gain, bank result early rather than risk regression


## OBSERVED RISKS (from benchmark analysis)

The following patterns have been observed to correlate with suboptimal outcomes:
- Multiple phys_opt_design rounds without interleaved WNS checks
- Strategy switch after a single failed attempt (at least 2 variations typically needed)
- Expensive place+route while timing is already improving on the current trajectory
- Continued exploration after reaching WNS target (risk of regression)
- Skipping rapidwright_report_timing for quick WNS estimates (it is ~5x faster than Vivado)
- PBLOCK on dense designs (>40% utilization) without adjusting the multiplier
- Calling vivado_report_timing_summary without first trying rapidwright_report_timing
- Keeping the same strategy for more than 3 rounds without measurable improvement


## PERFORMANCE OBSERVATIONS
- PBLOCK: historically highest observed success rate across benchmarks
- PhysOpt: moderate improvement, typically plateaus after 3 rounds
- Fanout: modest gains, only applicable when high-fanout nets on critical paths
- Fast timing: rapidwright_report_timing (~2.5s) vs Vivado (~14s)
- Cost: Worker=$0.005/call, Planner=$0.075/call (15x diff)


## ITERATION EFFICIENCY OBSERVATIONS
1. If WNS improves >0.020ns: continuing the current strategy may be productive
2. If no improvement after 2 attempts: switching strategy is a common pattern
3. If WNS target met: signalling DONE locks in gains
4. Prefer rapidwright_report_timing over Vivado for quick checks
5. Track remaining budget: cheaper actions when budget < 20%
