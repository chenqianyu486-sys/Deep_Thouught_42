## COMPETITION SCORING AWARENESS
Goal: maximize Score = alpha - 0.1*alpha*cost - 0.1*alpha*hours
- alpha (Fmax improvement) contributes positively; cost and hours subtract proportionally to alpha.
- WNS improvement is the primary lever: without WNS gain, no action generates positive score.
- But time and cost penalize proportionally to alpha: over-spending on expensive actions can offset WNS gains.
- rapidwright_report_timing (~2.5s) has lower cost/time impact than vivado_report_timing_summary (~14s).


## TIME AWARENESS

The optimizer has a wall-clock timeout (see Current Optimization State).
Each Vivado place+route cycle consumes the most time per action.
rapidwright_report_timing (~2.5s) has lower time impact than vivado_report_timing_summary (~14s).


## OBSERVED RISKS (from benchmark analysis)

The following patterns have been observed to correlate with suboptimal outcomes:
- Multiple phys_opt_design rounds without interleaved WNS checks
- Strategy switch after a single failed attempt correlates with lower final WNS
- Expensive place+route while timing is already improving on the current trajectory
- Continued exploration after reaching WNS target (risk of regression)
- Skipping rapidwright_report_timing for quick WNS estimates (it is ~5x faster than Vivado)
- PBLOCK on dense designs (>40% utilization) without adjusting the multiplier
- Calling vivado_report_timing_summary without first trying rapidwright_report_timing
- Keeping the same strategy for more than 3 rounds without measurable improvement


## PERFORMANCE OBSERVATIONS
- PBLOCK: historically highest observed success rate across benchmarks
- PhysOpt: moderate improvement, typically plateaus after 3 rounds
- Fanout: modest gains, only applicable when high-fanout nets on critical paths
- Fast timing: rapidwright_report_timing (~2.5s) vs Vivado (~14s)
- Cost: Worker=$0.005/call, Planner=$0.075/call (15x diff)
