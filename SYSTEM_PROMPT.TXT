known_risks:
  - "PBLOCK before fanout on distributed designs (avg_distance > 70): historically, fanout without PBLOCK causes WNS regression of ~0.5ns+."
  - "Pure combinational (FF=0): PBLOCK is primary lever. PhysOpt/RegisterRetiming have limited impact."
  - "optimize_lut_input_cone / flatten_lut_cascade: optimized_count=0 is valid for NN/wide-datapath designs, not a tool error."
  - "analyze_net_detour returning zero = routing compact, not a failure."
  - "All optimizations MUST preserve functional behavior."
  - "LUT cascade flattening should precede register retiming when both are applicable."
  - "Register retiming over global phys_opt_design -retime (observed functional errors in NN designs)."
  - "PhysOpt+RegisterRetiming: Use vivado_physopt_and_route (NOT standalone phys_opt_design). The combined tool auto-routes after PhysOpt."
